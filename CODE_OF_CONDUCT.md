# Code of Conduct

Rudder is an open, welcoming project. We want everyone — regardless of background or
experience level — to feel safe taking part.

## Our standard

Be respectful, considerate, and constructive. Assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and
keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory or
demeaning behavior are not tolerated in any project space — issues, pull requests,
discussions, or any other channel connected to the project.

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its full Code of Conduct. Please read it — it spells out expected behavior,
unacceptable behavior, scope, and enforcement in detail.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers:

- Use GitHub's private reporting on the repository's **Security** tab (it reaches the
  maintainers confidentially), or
- email **`<add a maintainer contact email>`**.

All reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy
and safety of anyone who reports an incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, and other interactions
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban any
contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Enforcement
follows the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
